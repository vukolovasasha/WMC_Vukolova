Internet Memes

What are memes?

Memes are images, videos, text, or ideas that spread rapidly across the internet.

They are usually humorous and are often shared through social media platforms.

History of memes

The word "meme" was introduced by biologist Richard Dawkins in 1976.

Internet memes became popular because people started sharing funny pictures, videos, and jokes online.

Functions of memes

  • Entertainment
  • Communication
  • Social commentary
  • Sharing emotions
  • Building online communities

Why are memes important?

Memes influence internet culture and help people communicate ideas quickly and creatively.
