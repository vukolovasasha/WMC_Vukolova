What are memes?

Memes are images, videos, texts, or ideas that spread quickly on the internet. They are usually humorous and are often shared on social media platforms.

How did memes appear?

The word "meme" was introduced by Richard Dawkins. Today, internet memes are a popular form of online communication and entertainment.

What is the function of memes?

Memes entertain people, express opinions, react to current events, and help users communicate emotions and ideas in a simple way.
